The Impact of Meetings

Meetings are costly but necessary—often called “the broccoli of work.” 🥦
When they lack intention, they drain energy. When curated well, they unlock collective brilliance!

28%

Of a Work Week

Over a quarter of the average work week is spent in meetings—taking time away from deep, focused work.

392 hrs

Hours Per Year

Nearly 10 full standard workweeks per employee spent inside virtual or physical conference rooms every year.

11M+

Meetings Every Day

Happening every single day. Imagine the creative momentum when those hours actually energize your team!

Sources: SpeakWise Meeting Statistics • National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER Working Paper w35706)

The Four Principles

A sneak peek at the whiteboard framework we practice together in our live coaching sessions! ✨

Want the full hands-on workshop? Let’s talk →
#1

The best meetings are curated experiences.

  • → Shape the tone & vibe.
  • → Never waste anyone’s time.
  • → Bring your personality to the table.
  • → Prioritize engagement.
#2

A meeting is NOT a prison.

  • → Understand that people have a choice!
  • → Center attendees.
  • → Know why it’s important to meet in the first place.
#3

The vibe has gravity.

  • → Be Authentic.
  • → Be Aware.
  • → Be Engaging.
  • → Be Respectful.
#4

Growth is a superpower.

  • → Have a Beginner’s Mindset.
  • → Always pause to reflect.
  • → Measure the experience against your intention.
  • → Give yourself grace.
